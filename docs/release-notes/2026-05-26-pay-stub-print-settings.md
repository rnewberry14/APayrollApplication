# Pay Stub Print Settings

Date: 2026-05-26

## Added

- New route `/payroll/paystub/{payrollRunEmployeeId}`.
- Configurable pay stub display settings.
- Company and employee address display.
- Earnings hours and rate display.
- Gross pay, pre-tax deductions, post-tax deductions, reimbursements, employee taxes, optional/internal employer taxes, and net pay.
- Direct deposit last 4 only and check number placeholder.
- YTD and accrual placeholders.
- State-required-fields checklist placeholder.
- NoAdviceDisclaimer on the printable page.

## Safety Notes

ClearPath Payroll does not provide legal, tax, financial, accounting, or payroll compliance advice. Review applicable pay statement requirements before distribution. Full SSNs and full bank account numbers are not displayed.
