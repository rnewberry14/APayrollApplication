# Printing Pay Stubs

## What this page does

The Pay Stub page displays printable demo pay statement details for one payroll run employee.

## When to use it

Use it after payroll has been calculated or approved.

## Before you begin

Have a demo payroll run with calculated employee check data.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open `/payroll/paystub/{payrollRunEmployeeId}`.
2. Review company name and address.
3. Review employee name, employee ID, and SSN last four only.
4. Review pay period and pay date.
5. Review earnings, hours, taxes, deductions, reimbursements, and net pay.
6. Review direct deposit last four or check number.
7. Review YTD placeholders.
8. Print to PDF.

## What to check

- Full SSN is not shown.
- Full bank account number is not shown.
- Required-field checklist is a placeholder.
- No compliance guarantee language appears.

## Common messages or errors

- Pay stub not found: confirm the payroll run employee ID.
- Missing line items: recalculate payroll/checks first.

## What this page does not do

It does not certify pay statement requirements or provide advice.

## Privacy/safety notes

Review applicable pay statement requirements before distribution. This is not legal, tax, financial, accounting, payroll compliance, or licensed professional advice.
