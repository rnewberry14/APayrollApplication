Pay Stub

What it does
- Displays a printable employee pay stub for a payroll run.
- Includes company name, employee name, last 4 of SSN, pay period, pay date, earnings, taxes, deductions, net pay, and direct deposit last 4.

Who can use it
- Payroll users or employees with permission to view individual pay stubs.

Step-by-step use
1. Open the pay stub page for a payroll run and employee.
2. Review the pay period, earnings, taxes, deductions, and net pay.
3. Confirm the direct deposit last 4 and employee name.
4. Click Print to generate a physical or PDF copy.

Required permissions
- Payroll stub view permissions or equivalent; only authorized users should access pay stubs.

Common errors
- "Pay stub not found": The payroll run or employee is not available for this pay stub.
- Missing company or employee data: Data must be present in the payroll run record.

Troubleshooting
- Verify the payroll run and employee IDs are correct.
- Ensure the payroll run contains employee calculation details.
- Check the employee has a verified direct deposit account for direct deposit last 4 to display.

Payroll/Tax/ACH/Security cautions
- Only show the last 4 digits of the SSN.
- Do not show full bank account or routing numbers.
- Use the print view only after reviewing payroll details for accuracy.
