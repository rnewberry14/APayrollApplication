# Pay Schedule Setup - User Guide

## Overview

Pay Schedule Setup lets authorized users create payroll calendars for a company. A pay schedule stores the schedule name, frequency, next pay date, next pay period dates, and active status.

## Who Can Use It

Payroll administrators and payroll managers who are allowed to manage company setup can use this page.

## Required Permissions

- View active companies.
- Create pay schedules for the selected company.
- View existing pay schedules for the selected company.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open **Pay Schedules** from the navigation menu.
2. Select the company.
3. Enter the schedule name.
4. Choose the frequency: Weekly, Biweekly, Semimonthly, or Monthly.
5. Enter the next pay date.
6. Enter the next period start and end dates.
7. Choose whether the schedule is active.
8. Review the calculated next pay period preview.
9. Click **Create Pay Schedule**.

Confirm pay schedule dates before processing payroll.

## Common Errors

| Error | Cause | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select a company | No company was selected | Choose an active company from the list |
| Name is required | The schedule name is blank | Enter a clear schedule name |
| End date before start date | Period dates are reversed | Correct the period start or end date |
| Pay date before period end | Pay date is earlier than the period end date | Confirm and correct the pay date |

## Payroll, Tax, ACH, and Security Cautions

- ClearPath Payroll provides software tools for payroll calculation, reporting, and data transmission. It does not provide legal, tax, financial, accounting, or payroll compliance advice. Users are responsible for verifying payroll, tax, filing, payment, and compliance requirements with qualified professionals or official agency sources.
- Pay schedule dates affect payroll run timing, tax calculation requests, and direct deposit timing.
- Do not enter bank account numbers, SSNs, tax IDs, or API secrets in pay schedule names.
- Confirm dates before approving payroll or submitting ACH/direct deposit.
