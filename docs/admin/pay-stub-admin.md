Pay Stub - Admin Guide

What it does
- Provides administrators with a printable pay stub view for a payroll run employee.

Who can use it
- Payroll administrators and managers.

Step-by-step use
1. Navigate to the employee pay stub page for the desired payroll run.
2. Confirm the company name, employee name, pay period, and pay date.
3. Review earnings, employee taxes, deductions, and net pay.
4. Verify direct deposit last 4 and confirm payroll accuracy.
5. Use the Print button to generate a physical or electronic record.

Required permissions
- Administrator or payroll manager role required to access individual pay stubs.

Common issues
- Missing employee calculation data: ensure the payroll run was calculated.
- Pay stub cannot load: check payroll run status and employee assignment.

Operational notes
- Pay stubs are read-only and designed for printing only.
- Year-to-date totals are currently placeholders and may be updated in future releases.

Security cautions
- Do not expose full SSN or full bank account numbers.
- Restrict pay stub access to authorized payroll staff only.
